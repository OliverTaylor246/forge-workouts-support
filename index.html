Help & support

Log your first workout

In Train, choose a starter routine or tap + to create one. Start the workout, enter your reps and weight, then tap the checkmark for each completed set. Tap Finish and save to add the session to History and Progress.

Import a workout from ChatGPT

In Library, tap Create ChatGPT prompt and copy it into your chat. Share the resulting workout text or JSON file from your iPhone, select Forge (under More if needed), and choose a folder. Open Forge after saving. You can also copy the response and use Library → Paste workout from ChatGPT.

Back up your training

Open Library → Export full backup and save the JSON file to Files or a location you control. To move your log to another iPhone, install Forge there and use Library → Restore a backup. Restoring replaces the current log.

Get help

Beta testers can send feedback privately through TestFlight. For other questions or bug reports, use the Forge support tracker. Issues are public. Share your app version and what happened, and keep personal information and backup files out of public reports.
