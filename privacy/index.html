Forge

Privacy policy

Effective November 15, 2026

Workout data

Forge stores your routines, folders, sets, notes, and training history in its local storage on your iPhone. Forge has no account, app server, advertising, or in-app tracking. Forge does not send your training log to its developer.

Camera, photos, and notifications

If you use the plate calculator, Forge processes the photo you take or select on your device to read plate labels. It uses the photo for the calculation and does not add a copy to your workout history. Forge uses local notifications for rest timers if you allow them. You can change camera and notification access in iPhone Settings.

Imports, sharing, and backups

Forge parses workout text and JSON on your device. If you choose to load a public ChatGPT share link, Forge contacts the site to read the page. The site receives normal web request information and its privacy policy applies. Forge does not send your training history with that request. If you share a workout or export a backup, the destination you choose receives the file or text.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you remove it or delete the app. Copies you exported and copies held by iPhone backup services are managed where they were saved. Forge has no server copy to delete.

Support and site hosting

If you send a report through TestFlight or GitHub, Forge's developer uses the information you choose to submit to respond and investigate. Apple and GitHub handle those services under their own policies. GitHub Pages hosts this site. For privacy questions or help deleting your data, use the options on the support page.
