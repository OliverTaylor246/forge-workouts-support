Bell

Privacy policy

Effective November 22, 2026

Workout data

Bell stores your routines, folders, sets, notes, and training history in its local storage on your iPhone. Bell has no advertising or tracking. You can use its local training log without signing in. Optional community features use your Apple iCloud account.

Profiles and optional body context

You can use a local profile or deliberately create a Bell profile with your existing iCloud account. The private cloud profile stores your chosen nickname. Bell does not collect a new password or email for this flow. Optional height, bodyweight, activity level, goals, experience, and equipment preferences are stored locally on your iPhone. Bell does not put these body measurements into its CloudKit profile records. Full workout backups include saved private profile context. You can review a separate context export before sharing it with an assistant or another destination.

Public profile publishing is optional. Bell shows a preview containing only your nickname and the PRs you select. Anyone using the community can read that published snapshot, including the selected exercise names, weights, reps, dates, and whether a record was entered manually or came from the training log. Publishing does not include height, bodyweight, activity level, or private notes. Public PRs update only when you publish again. Make profile private removes the public profile snapshot.

Camera, photos, and notifications

If you use the plate calculator, Bell processes the photo you take or select on your device to read plate labels. It uses the photo for the calculation and does not add a copy to your workout history. Bell uses local notifications for rest timers if you allow them. You can change camera and notification access in iPhone Settings.

Imports, sharing, and backups

Bell parses workout text and JSON on your device. If you choose to load a public ChatGPT share link, Bell contacts the site to read the page. The site receives normal web request information and its privacy policy applies. Bell does not send your training history with that request. If you share a workout or export a backup, the destination you choose receives the file or text.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you remove it or delete the app. Copies you exported and copies held by iPhone backup services are managed where they were saved. You can remove published templates using Make private. Turning friend sharing off deletes your current shared result channel and revokes its friend code. Public usage and abuse reports may remain in CloudKit for operation and moderation. In Profile & privacy, Delete Bell account removes its private cloud profile and associated public profiles, published templates, owned friend feeds, usage records, and reports. It clears this device’s profile and turns off friend sharing and community usage participation while retaining local workout history. A separate Erase local profile action clears only this device’s profile and body context, without changing cloud publications or friend sharing. Cloud deletion is available even when using a local profile. Deletion is complete only after the cloud operations succeed. Using locally hides the public profile and stops friend results, while retaining the private cloud nickname for restoration and previously published templates. These actions do not delete your Apple account. For deletion requests, use the support contact.

Friends and public workouts

If you enable friend sharing, Bell uploads an encrypted result feed to Apple CloudKit. It contains your display name and up to 30 shared workout names, dates, set counts, personal-best counts, and effort ratings. The friend code includes the key to read the feed. Anyone you give that code can read it. Your private workout notes and individual set weights are excluded from the friend feed.

If you publish a workout or folder, its templates and instructions are public. Bell previews this information before you publish. CloudKit associates records with an app-specific iCloud identity. If you explicitly enable community usage participation, Bell creates one public participation record per program you use, tied to your app identity. It contains the program identifier and CloudKit service metadata, without weights, reps, or an explicit session timestamp. Repeating the program does not update the record. Counts describe participating lifters and can undercount total use. Turning participation off stops future records; deleting Bell cloud data removes your existing participation records. Public workout reports include the template identifier and reason, and are available to the developer for moderation.

Optional beta usage counts

Usage capture is off unless you enable it. Bell can keep local timestamps and counts for app opens, imports, selected-context exports, reviewed revisions, and completed workouts under a random installation identifier. These events exclude exercise names, working weights, reps, body measurements, notes, account identifiers, and conversation contents. Initial-launch metadata is stored locally to distinguish a new installation from an existing one. A usage file can be reviewed and voluntarily shared with the beta organizer. Bell does not upload it automatically. You can turn capture off and erase local counts in Library. Late consent, interruptions, or truncated logs are marked incomplete so growth reports do not treat unknown use as zero.

Optional ChatGPT connection

Bell Connection is an optional private ChatGPT Sites service. Signing in uses your ChatGPT account, separately from your iCloud Bell profile. Opening the connection does not upload your workouts. You choose and review a training snapshot, then separately allow reading the selection, shared folder names, and incoming drafts. Private notes and body/profile context start off. When explicitly included, the uploaded snapshot can contain selected exercise names, dates, logged loads, reps or seconds, routine templates, and the additional context shown in the preview.

OpenAI Sites authenticates the caller and hosts the service with Cloudflare storage. Records are scoped to the signed-in Site user. The connected assistant can read only the current permitted snapshot and stage draft plans; it cannot directly change workouts on the phone. Bell validates and previews incoming plans before saving. Drafts download when you open the connection screen. A save or undo receipt is sent only after the local operation succeeds.

Snapshot access expires after seven days. Previews and drafts expire within one day or with the snapshot, whichever comes first. Expired records are removed when the portal next opens or updates. Delete connection data in the portal removes your stored snapshot, previews, and drafts now. Signing out, uninstalling a plugin, or deleting the separate iCloud Bell profile does not delete this connection's records. Delete them in the portal while signed into the same ChatGPT account. Copies already provided to an assistant remain subject to that provider's controls and policies. Bell does not upload full chat conversations or require an AI API key for this connection.

AI explanations

Optional AI explanations use Apple Intelligence on your device when available. Bell supplies the exercise name and a target computed from your logged sets to the on-device model.

Support and site hosting

If you send a report through TestFlight or GitHub, Bell's developer uses the information you choose to submit to respond and investigate. Apple and GitHub handle those services under their own policies. GitHub Pages hosts this site. For privacy questions or help deleting your data, use the options on the support page.
