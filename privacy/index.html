Bell

Privacy policy

Effective November 22, 2026

Workout data

Bell stores your routines, folders, sets, notes, and training history in its local storage on your iPhone. Bell has no advertising or tracking. You can use its local training log without signing in. Optional community features use your Apple iCloud account.

Camera, photos, and notifications

If you use the plate calculator, Bell processes the photo you take or select on your device to read plate labels. It uses the photo for the calculation and does not add a copy to your workout history. Bell uses local notifications for rest timers if you allow them. You can change camera and notification access in iPhone Settings.

Imports, sharing, and backups

Bell parses workout text and JSON on your device. If you choose to load a public ChatGPT share link, Bell contacts the site to read the page. The site receives normal web request information and its privacy policy applies. Bell does not send your training history with that request. If you share a workout or export a backup, the destination you choose receives the file or text.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you remove it or delete the app. Copies you exported and copies held by iPhone backup services are managed where they were saved. You can remove published templates using Make private. Turning friend sharing off deletes your current shared result channel and revokes its friend code. Public usage and abuse reports may remain in CloudKit for operation and moderation. For deletion requests, use the support contact.

Friends and public workouts

If you enable friend sharing, Bell uploads an encrypted result feed to Apple CloudKit. It contains your display name and up to 30 shared workout names, dates, set counts, personal-best counts, and effort ratings. The friend code includes the key to read the feed. Anyone you give that code can read it. Your private workout notes and individual set weights are excluded from the friend feed.

If you publish a workout or folder, its templates and instructions are public. Bell previews this information before you publish. CloudKit associates records with an app-specific iCloud identity. Bell stores a pseudonymous usage record when you finish an imported public workout so its creator can see a distinct-user count. Public workout reports include the template identifier and reason, and are available to the developer for moderation.

AI explanations

Optional AI explanations use Apple Intelligence on your device when available. Bell supplies the exercise name and a target computed from your logged sets to the on-device model.

Support and site hosting

If you send a report through TestFlight or GitHub, Bell's developer uses the information you choose to submit to respond and investigate. Apple and GitHub handle those services under their own policies. GitHub Pages hosts this site. For privacy questions or help deleting your data, use the options on the support page.
